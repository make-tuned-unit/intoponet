Privacy

Privacy policy

intoponet is built so that your browsing belongs to you. What you read, what you save and what you type are kept on your own Mac. We do not receive them, and there is no account to tie them to. The one thing that becomes part of what everyone sees is a Beacon, and only when you choose to publish one.

This page says exactly what leaves your Mac, when, and to whom. Where something is not perfect we say so. intoponet is open source, so every statement here can be checked against the code.

In short

  • Your history, bookmarks, library, notes, saved passwords and knowledge stay on your Mac, encrypted. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no sale of data and no profile of you.
  • The app contains no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Beacons are public, and are shared only when you publish one after seeing a preview.
  • The app has to contact some services to do its work, such as a news outlet’s feed. They are listed below. Each of them sees your network address, as any site you visit does.
  • This website counts visits without cookies and without storing network addresses.

What stays on your Mac

  • History, bookmarks, pins, collections, captures and saved pages. Stored in your user folder and encrypted with a key kept in your macOS Keychain.
  • Saved passwords. Encrypted the same way.
  • What you type. Suggestions in the address bar come from your own history. A search is sent to DuckDuckGo only when you press Enter.
  • Translation. Done on your Mac. The text being translated does not leave it.
  • Laya and Tidy. Optional, and run on your Mac. The pages you absorb and the questions you ask stay there.
  • Private browsing. Leaves no history and forgets site sign-ins when it ends.

Because this is all kept on your Mac, we cannot see it, recover it for you, or hand it to anyone. If you use backups such as Time Machine, your intoponet data is in them unless you turn on the option in Settings that leaves it out.

What you can choose to share

Beacons

A Beacon is a page you recommend to everyone. Beacons are in private beta. Nothing is published unless you press Publish after seeing a preview of exactly what will be shared: the page’s address with tracking parameters removed, its title, a topic, an optional short note and an optional place rounded to about 10 km.

A Beacon is not tied to an account or to your name. Each device signs its Beacons with a random key so that you can withdraw them. To limit abuse, the registry counts publications per network using a hash of the network address that changes every day; it does not keep the address itself. You can withdraw a Beacon at any time from the app. The full rules and retention periods are in the Beacon policy.

Your visits, history and private notes are never contributed automatically, and reading other people’s Beacons does not tell us where you are or what you are looking at: the app fetches the same public list for everyone.

Feedback and ideas

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your rating, your comment, the app’s version and the country the request came from. If you send an idea from this website, we receive what you wrote and, if you give them, your name and email address so that we can reply. Please do not put anything in a comment that you would not want us to read.

No list catches everything: hosts that are not on it, and a site’s own advertising and measurement, still load.

What this website records

intoponet.com uses its own small visit counter, with no cookies and no outside analytics company. For each page view it records the page, the site that linked to you, the country, whether the visit looks automated, and campaign labels in the link if there are any. A random number kept only for the life of the browser tab groups the pages of one visit. To count visitors without knowing who they are, it makes a fingerprint from your browser’s description and language, mixed with a secret and the date, so it changes every day and cannot be turned back into anything about you. Your network address and your browser’s description are not stored. We read these records with our own reporting tool, which keeps a copy.

A download of the app is counted the same way, and we get an email saying that a copy was downloaded and from which country. Feedback sent from the app is recorded in the same store, including the first 256 characters of the comment.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, the visit counter records nothing for you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not collect your browsing history, your searches, your bookmarks, your passwords or the content of pages you read.
  • We do not use what you do in the app to train AI models.

Your choices and your rights

  • You can turn off the daily update check, leave Beacons unused, and never send feedback. The browser works without any of them.
  • You can ask what we hold that relates to you, and ask us to correct or delete it. In practice that is an email you sent us or a Beacon you published; we hold nothing else that identifies you.
  • You can complain to your privacy regulator. In Canada that is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada; in the European Union or the United Kingdom, your national data protection authority.

To do any of this, or to ask a question, email support@atlasatlantic.co. We answer within 30 days.

Children

intoponet is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
