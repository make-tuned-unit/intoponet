03 / Private knowledge. Shared discovery.

Your curiosity
belongs to you.
Your discoveries
can belong to all of us.

A more human internet starts with a choice.

Exploring shouldn’t mean broadcasting your life. Your personal library stays on your device. When you want to contribute, a Beacon lets you deliberately share a public pointer with the community.

04 / The horizon

A web we explore together.
A future we build together.

We’re building toward a shared field of human interest: a way to discover more of the web through places, context and intentional contributions. An internet that gives curiosity somewhere new to go.

Our longer-term vision is a community-operated commons, with independent nodes and open participation. Beyond the browser, community-owned connectivity is a separate ambition—one to earn through practical, local pilots.
