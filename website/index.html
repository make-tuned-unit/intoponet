01 / A living view of the web

Actual prototype · illustrative snapshot

The world is moving.
Get a sense of it.

Start with Earth. Follow a place, a story, a question. Worldwide news and public sources form an explorable field of attention, with real websites just a click away.

01

See the momentum.

Follow rising and fading news coverage across the world. RSS reporting adds perspective beyond what people are reading on Wikipedia.

02

Look behind the headline.

The Olds connects today’s stories with sourced historical context. Explore the timeline, inspect the evidence, and see where context is still limited.

03

Make knowledge your own.

Keep bookmarks, notes and collections in your personal library. Optional local intelligence helps you find connections in what you save.

02 / Private knowledge. Shared discovery.

Your curiosity
belongs to you.
Your discoveries
can belong to all of us.

A more human internet starts with a choice.

Exploring shouldn’t mean broadcasting your life. Your personal library stays on your device. When you want to contribute, a Beacon lets you deliberately share a public pointer with the community.

Discover privately↓Choose what to share↓Publish a Beacon ✳

Beacons are in private beta. Your visits and private notes are not automatic public contributions.

03 / The horizon

A web we explore together.
A future we build together.

We’re building toward a shared field of human interest: a way to discover more of the web through places, context and intentional contributions. An internet that gives curiosity somewhere new to go.

Our longer-term vision is a community-operated commons, with independent nodes and open participation. Beyond the browser, community-owned connectivity is a separate ambition—one to earn through practical, local pilots.
